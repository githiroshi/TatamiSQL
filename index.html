CORE FEATURES

Make SQL more intuitive with mouse controls.

01

Build JOINs with your mouse

Double-click a table name and choose a suggestion. Add a JOIN clause with its condition to your SQL.

02

Choose columns from a list

Double-click the asterisk in SELECT *. Choose the columns you need, and they are applied to the SELECT clause.

03

Preview cell contents

Double-click a cell to view its contents in a larger window. Easily inspect long text and JSON.

04

Group table names

Group tables by shared names. Keep large schemas organized and easier to scan.
